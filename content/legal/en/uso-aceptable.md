# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This policy explains which uses of the **Agent CEO** service (the "Service")
are permitted and which are not. It is published by **Klinworks, LLC**
("Klinworks," "we," "us"), a Delaware (United States) limited liability
company, file no. 10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental
Drive, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713.

It applies to every person or organization that uses the Service, to the
members they invite to their organization, and to **the agents they
configure**: anything an agent does within your organization counts as done by
you. It expands on sections 4 and 5 of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms),
which form part of it; if anything conflicts, the Terms prevail.

---

## 1. The underlying idea

Agent CEO exists so you can run **your legitimate business** with a team of AI
agents. Agents perform real actions: they send messages, publish content, and
touch your systems. That is why this policy prohibits not only content but
also **conduct** that an agent can repeat at scale in seconds.

If you are unsure whether a use is permitted, ask us first at
**legal@klinworks.com**.

## 2. Unwanted communications and spam

When you use the Service to contact people, **you are the sender** (Terms
§5.7). You may not:

- Send unsolicited commercial communications or **bulk messages to people who
  have not consented**, by email, SMS, WhatsApp, social media, or any other
  channel.
- Use WhatsApp in breach of Meta's policies for the WhatsApp Business
  Platform, including templates, prior recipient consent, and messaging
  limits.
- Violate applicable anti-spam laws, such as the U.S. **CAN-SPAM Act** and the
  **Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA)** where they apply, or their
  equivalents in your country.
- Ignore an unsubscribe request, conceal the sender's identity, or use
  misleading subject lines and headers.
- Use purchased, scraped, or unknown-source lists without a legal basis for
  contacting those people.

## 3. Impersonation, deception, and fraud

You may not:

- **Impersonate** a person, company, brand, or government body, or create
  communications that appear to come from someone who did not send them.
- Fabricate documents, reviews, testimonials, or evidence and present them as
  genuine.
- Use the Service for scams, phishing, pyramid schemes, deceptive collection
  of credentials or payment details, or any other form of fraud.
- **Clone a person's voice or likeness without their express permission**, or
  portray them saying or doing something they did not say or do.
- Hide from your customers that they are talking to an AI agent when the law
  requires you to tell them.

## 4. Illegal or harmful content

You may not generate, store, or distribute with the Service:

- **Material that exploits or sexualizes minors**, in any form. We report it
  to the competent authorities.
- Content that is illegal in the jurisdiction where you operate or where it is
  distributed.
- Content that incites violence or promotes terrorism or hatred against people
  based on their origin, religion, gender, orientation, disability, or other
  protected characteristic.
- **Harassment**, threats, intimidation, or disclosure of other people's
  personal data to harm someone.
- **Election misinformation**: false content about when, where, or how to
  vote, about candidates or results, or designed to discourage voting.
- Other misinformation intended to cause harm, including health
  misinformation or content that impersonates news media.

## 5. Intellectual property

You may not use the Service to **infringe copyrights**, trademarks, trade
secrets, or other third-party rights: for example, copying and publishing
others' works without a license or selling products bearing trademarks that do
not belong to you. If you believe someone is infringing your rights with
content hosted on the Service, follow the procedure in the
[DMCA Policy](/en/legal/dmca).

## 6. Security of the Service and of third parties

You may not:

- Distribute **malware**, viruses, ransomware, or any code designed to damage,
  spy on, or take control of systems.
- **Circumvent the Service's limits**: credits, agent approval controls, usage
  limits, or your plan's restrictions, including creating multiple accounts to
  obtain free credits.
- **Circumvent or probe security**: attempt to access other organizations'
  data, test vulnerabilities without authorization, or interfere with the
  operation of the Service. If you find a flaw, report it as described in the
  [Security Policy](/en/legal/security).
- Launch denial-of-service attacks or deliberately overload the
  infrastructure.
- Engage in **abusive scraping**: extracting data from third-party sites or
  services without a legal basis, bypassing their terms or technical measures,
  or at a rate that impairs their operation.
- Use the Service to train competing models or resell it without a written
  agreement with us.

## 7. Decisions about people

You may not use agents to make **decisions with legal or similarly significant
effects on people**—hiring or firing, granting or denying credit, insurance,
housing, healthcare, or education—**without effective human oversight** and
without complying with the laws that apply to you, including Article 22 of the
GDPR (Terms §4.2 and Privacy Policy §9).

An agent may prepare information for such a decision; the decision is made,
and answered for, by a person.

## 8. Your responsibility for agents

- Configure autonomy levels and approvals in proportion to the risk (Terms
  §5.2).
- Review what agents produce before using it in decisions that matter (Terms
  §5.3).
- You are responsible for what you publish or send to third parties with the
  Service (Terms §5.6 and §5.7).

The fact that an action was performed by an agent **does not excuse it** from
this policy.

## 9. What happens if this policy is breached

Depending on the severity and recurrence, we may:

- **Warn you** and ask you to correct the use.
- Remove or disable the affected content, agent, or integration.
- Temporarily **suspend** the account or organization.
- Permanently **terminate** access (Terms §9.2).
- Report the facts to the authorities when the law requires it or there is a
  risk to people.

Where reasonable, we will notify you before acting. **In the face of a serious
or imminent risk**—to people, to third parties, or to the Service—we may act
immediately (Terms §4.3). If you believe we have made a mistake, write to us at
legal@klinworks.com and we will review the decision.

Suspension or termination of access for breaching this policy does not entitle
you to a refund of credits already consumed (Terms §3.5).
