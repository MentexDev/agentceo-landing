# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

**Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we," "us") wants everyone to be able to use
the **Agent CEO** website (agentceo.io), whether or not they have a
disability, and whatever device or assistive technology they use. This
statement explains the goal we have set, what we have measured, what we know
still fails, and how to let us know.

Klinworks is a Delaware (United States) limited liability company, file no.
10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305,
Newark, Delaware 19713.

This statement covers the public website agentceo.io. The application
(app.agentceo.io) was not included in the measurements for this version.

---

## 1. Our goal

We follow the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA**,
the international reference standard.

## 2. What we have measured

On **October 20, 2026**, we reviewed the home page and the internal pages, on
desktop and on mobile:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Automated audit with axe-core | No violations on the pages reviewed |
| Lighthouse, Accessibility category | 100 on the pages measured |

## 3. What already works

- **Keyboard navigation** throughout the website.
- A **"Skip to content"** link at the start of every page.
- Respect for the system's **"reduce motion"** preference.
- Content can be **read without JavaScript**.
- Color **contrast** reviewed and corrected.
- Accessible **language selector** and **website assistant**.

## 4. Known limitations

We are honest about what is not right yet:

- **Animated demos.** Some imitate screens from the application. They are
  **illustrative**: the information they convey is also in the page text.
- **Automatic carousels.** Every carousel that advances on its own has a
  visible pause and play button next to its arrows (WCAG 2.2.2). They also stop
  on mouse hover, on keyboard focus, or on touch, and they do not move if your
  system asks for reduced motion.

## 5. What an automated audit cannot see

Automated tools detect only some accessibility issues. **They do not replace
testing with real users**, including people who use screen readers,
magnifiers, or voice control. That is why your feedback matters to us.

## 6. Tell us if something does not work for you

If you encounter a barrier, or need content in another format, write to us at
**info@klinworks.com** and tell us:

- The page (web address) where it happens.
- What you were trying to do and what happened.
- Your browser, your device, and, if you use one, your assistive technology.

We will respond within [PENDIENTE: committed time frame] and, while we fix it,
we will find another way to give you the information or service you need.

## 7. Review of this statement

We will measure again when we change the design or add major sections, and we
will update this statement with the date and the results.
