# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This policy explains what personal data **Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we,"
"us") processes when providing the **AgentCEO** service (the "Service"), for
what purposes, with whom we share it, and what rights you have.

Klinworks is a Delaware (United States) limited liability company, File No.
10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305,
Newark, Delaware 19713.

**Privacy contact: privacidad@klinworks.com**

---

## 1. Two Different Roles, and Why It Matters

We process personal data in two different capacities:

**1.1 As a controller**, with respect to the data of the people who use the
Service: your account, your billing, your support, your use of the platform.
We decide what it is used for.

**1.2 As a processor**, with respect to the data you enter about **your own
customers, contacts and suppliers**. There, you decide: we only process it
following your instructions in order to provide the Service to you. **You are
the controller of that data** and are responsible for having a legal basis to
process it.

This distinction determines whom a person wishing to exercise their rights
should contact: if they are your customer, you; if they are a user of the
Service, us.

## 2. What Data We Process

**2.1 Your account data.** Full name, email address, phone number (optional),
encrypted password, and two-factor authentication factors if you enable it.

**2.2 Your organization's data.** Company name, brands, team members and their
roles, and the configuration of your agents.

**2.3 Content you enter.** Documents, conversations with the agents, tasks,
generated deliverables and everything the Service produces for you.

**2.4 Data about your customers and contacts.** If you use the customer, sales
or support modules, you will process third-party data: name, email, phone
number, order history and conversations. We process it as a processor (Section
1.2).

**2.5 Integration credentials.** When you connect an external system, we store
its keys or tokens, **always encrypted**, together with the identifier and
email address of the connected account.

**2.6 Billing data.** Subscribed plan, credit history and transactions. **We do
not store full card numbers**: payment is processed by a specialized provider.

**2.7 Technical data.** IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, active
sessions and error logs.

**2.8 Activity logs.** We keep a log of changes to your data so you can audit
and restore what your agents did.

**2.9 Data from this website (agentceo.io).** If you join the waitlist: your
email address, the page language, the campaign that brought you (UTM or click
parameters), the first page you viewed, the origin of the site that linked to
you, your browser (user-agent), your type of business if you tell us, and the
version of the privacy notice you saw when signing up. If you request a
resource: your email address and the resource. The website's AI assistant
**does not store your conversations on our servers**: they live only in your
browser for 7 days; to limit abuse, we count messages per day using a **hash**
of your IP address, without storing the IP address in plain text. **This website
does not use analytics or advertising cookies.**

**We do not deliberately process special categories of data** (health, ethnic
origin, political opinions, biometrics). If you enter them in your content, you
do so under your own responsibility and must have a legal basis for it.

## 4. What We Do NOT Do With Your Data

**4.1 We do not train AI models on your content.** Neither our own nor third
parties'. Our contracts with model providers prohibit them from using the data
we send them on your behalf for training.

**4.2 We do not read your content for other purposes.** We only access it if
you ask us to in order to resolve an issue, if required by law, or if it is
essential to investigate serious abuse.

**4.3 We do not record personal data in our technical logs.** Logs store
identifiers, not identities: never email addresses, phone numbers or full
names.

## 7. How Long We Retain Data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Active account and content | For as long as your account is open |
| Content after the account is closed | **30 days** to export, then deletion |
| Items in the trash | **30 days**, then permanent purge |
| Change log | **30 days** |
| Security and access logs | Up to 12 months |
| Billing data | The period required by applicable tax law |
| Integration credentials | Until you disconnect the integration |

Backups follow their own rotation cycle and may retain data for a few
additional weeks before being overwritten.

## 8. Your Rights

Regardless of where you live, you may exercise these rights by writing to
**privacidad@klinworks.com**:

- **Access**: find out what data we hold about you.
- **Rectification**: correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure**: ask us to delete it.
- **Restriction**: restrict its processing in certain cases.
- **Portability**: receive it in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Objection**: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdrawal of consent**: at any time, without retroactive effect.
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not treat you worse for exercising your
  rights.

**Response time:** up to 30 days, extendable by another 30 days when the
request is complex, with prior notice to you.

**Verification:** to protect your data, we may ask you to verify your identity
before handling the request.

**Complaints:** if you believe we have not honored your rights, you may lodge a
complaint with the supervisory authority in your country. In Spain, this is the
Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos,
aepd.es).

The specific rights under the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA are detailed in the
[GDPR and CCPA Notice](/en/legal/gdpr).

## 9. Automated Decision-Making

The Service performs automated actions through AI agents on your business,
according to the configuration you set. **We do not make automated decisions
that produce legal or similarly significant effects on the users of the
Service.**

If you configure agents that make decisions about individuals — hiring,
granting credit, personalized pricing — **you are the controller of that
processing** and must comply with Article 22 of the GDPR and equivalent
regulations: provide information, ensure human intervention and allow the
decision to be contested. The Terms of Service prohibit doing so without
effective human oversight.

## 10. Security

We encrypt data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Integration credentials are
encrypted with keys that not even our technical staff can read from the
database. Isolation between organizations is enforced at both the database and
application levels.

The details are in the [Security Policy](/en/legal/security).

**Breach notification.** If a security breach occurs that poses a risk to your
rights, we will inform you **without undue delay and within a maximum of 72
hours** of becoming aware of it, and we will notify the supervisory authority
where required.

## 11. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age, and we do not
knowingly collect their data; in particular, we comply with the U.S. COPPA law
with respect to children under 13. If we detect an account belonging to a
minor, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us with data,
write to us at privacidad@klinworks.com.

## 12. Cookies

We use only **strictly necessary or functional** cookies and local storage, as
described in the [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/cookies). There is no analytics or
advertising.

## 12A. U.S. Residents and the GPC Signal

**We do not sell or share** personal information (as those terms are defined
under California law). We honor your browser's **Global Privacy Control (GPC)**
signal as a request to opt out of sale or sharing, and you can make the same
request at [Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information](/en/do-not-sell-or-share/).
Details for California are in the
[Notice to California Residents](/en/legal/california) and, for other states,
in [Privacy Rights in Other U.S. States](/en/legal/state-privacy).

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update it. If the change is material — a new purpose, a subprocessor
that processes personal data, a longer retention period — we will notify you by
email or within the Service at least **30 days** in advance. The date in the
header always indicates the latest version.
