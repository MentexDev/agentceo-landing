---

## 1. What a subprocessor is

A **subprocessor** is a company we engage to process personal data on our
behalf while we provide the Service to you: for example, the company that hosts
the database, processes payments, or runs the language models your agents use.

They do not decide what the data is used for: they process it only for the
specific task we assign to them.

## 2. All of them process data under contract

Each active subprocessor processes data **under a contract** that requires it
to:

- Use the data only to provide its service to us, never for its own purposes.
- Protect the data with appropriate security measures.
- Maintain data protection obligations equivalent to ours.

With language model providers, we additionally contract that they **will not
use** the data we send them on your behalf **for training** (Privacy Policy
§4.1).

## 5. Your right to object

If you have a DPA and object to a new subprocessor on reasonable grounds
related to data protection, write to us at **privacidad@klinworks.com** within
the notice period explaining the reason.

We will seek a solution in good faith: for example, preventing that provider
from processing your data or offering you an alternative. If that is not
possible, you may cancel the affected Service in accordance with the
[Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms) (§9.1).

## 6. Where data is processed

Klinworks is located in the United States, and most subprocessors process data
there. The safeguards for international transfers are explained in the
[GDPR and CCPA Notice](/en/legal/gdpr) (§4) and in the DPA.
