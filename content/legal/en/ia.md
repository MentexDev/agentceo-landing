# Use of Artificial Intelligence

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

explains, in plain language, how we use it, what you control, which providers
are involved, and the limits we set for ourselves. It is published by
**Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we," "us"), which operates the website

[Security Policy](/en/legal/security).

## 1. What Agent CEO's agents do

Agent CEO uses **AI agents that perform real tasks** in your business: creating
and modifying records, preparing content, contacting your customers, or
working with the systems you connect. They do so **only with the permissions
you configure**.

## 2. You stay in control

- **Approval for what matters.** Anything sensitive or irreversible—for
  example, **spending money**—requires your approval before it is executed.
- **Blocked by default.** An action that has no policy assigned to it **is
  blocked**: when in doubt, it is not executed.
- **Least privilege.** We recommend granting each agent only what it needs
  (section 6.2 of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms)).

Configuring these controls is your responsibility, and if you authorize an
agent to act without approval, the outcome of those actions is yours (section
5.2 of the Terms).

## 3. AI makes mistakes: review what matters

AI models can produce **incorrect, incomplete, or fabricated** results, even
when they sound confident. **Review the results** before using them in legal,
financial, tax, medical, or security decisions. Nothing an agent produces is
professional advice.

## 4. Which models and providers we use

| Purpose | Providers |
|---|---|
| Language models that run the agents | OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), Anthropic, and xAI |
| Voice (recognition and synthesis) | Deepgram and ElevenLabs |

We may change the model behind a feature while seeking to maintain equivalent
quality. The current list of providers that process personal data is on the
[Subprocessors](/en/legal/subprocessors) page.

## 5. Your data does not train models

**We do not train AI models on your business content**, whether our own or
third parties' models, and **our contracts with model providers prohibit them**
from using the data we send them on your behalf for training.

## 6. AI-generated content

The Service can generate text, **images, and videos**. To the extent we hold
rights in that content, they are yours; but if you publish it, **you are the
one responsible** for it toward third parties (sections 5.6 and 7.4 of the
[Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms)).

**Voice or likeness clones.** They are created only **with the express
permission of the person who owns** that voice or likeness, and that
permission **can be revoked** at any time. Using them to impersonate someone or
to pass off content as real when it is not is prohibited (section 4.2 of the
Terms).

## 7. The website assistant is an AI, and it tells you so

The assistant that answers questions on agentceo.io **is an artificial
intelligence, not a person**, and it says so, in accordance with California's
**SB 1001** and **Article 50 of the EU AI Act**.

Today the assistant **answers only with content published on our website** and
**does not use a paid language model**. Your conversations with it **are not
stored on our server**: they stay in your browser for 7 days. If in the future
it starts using a language model, **we will update this document** before
doing so.

## 8. Prohibited uses

In addition to what section 4.2 of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms)
prohibits, **you may not use the Service to make decisions with legal or
similarly significant effects on people**—hiring, credit, insurance,
healthcare—**without effective human oversight**. If you configure agents that
take part in such decisions, you are responsible for that processing and for
complying with applicable law (section 9 of the
[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy)).

## 9. Changes to this document

We will update it when the models, the providers, or the way we use AI change.
The date at the top indicates the latest version.
