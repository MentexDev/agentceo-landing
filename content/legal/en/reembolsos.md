# Refunds and Cancellation

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This policy explains how charges, cancellation, and refunds work for the
**Agent CEO** service (the "Service"), provided by **Klinworks, LLC**
("Klinworks," "we," "us"), a Delaware (United States) limited liability
company, file no. 10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental
Drive, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713.

It applies to every person or organization that subscribes to a plan or buys
credits. It expands on section 3 of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms); if
anything conflicts, the Terms prevail.

not roll over to the following week and **are not refunded**.

## 2. Canceling your plan

- You can **cancel whenever you want** from within the Service, with no
  penalty.
- **You keep access until the end of the paid period** (Terms §9.1). You will
  not be charged again afterward.
- Canceling does not delete your data immediately: after termination, you have
  **30 days** to export your content (Terms §9.3).

## 3. When refunds apply and when they do not

These are the rules, all **except as required by applicable law**:

**3.1 Your first subscription: 7 days.** If this is the **first time** you
subscribe to a paid plan and you have used **less than 10% of its credits**,
you may request a full refund of that first payment within **7 days** of
subscribing.

**3.2 Credit packs: 14 days.** The **Chispa, Envión, and Turbina** packs are
refunded if you request it within **14 days** of purchase and **you have not
used any of their credits**. [PENDIENTE: confirm whether Reserva follows the
same rule]

**3.3 Credits already used.** Outside the two cases above, credits that have
already been used **are not refunded**, because they correspond to computing
capacity already consumed with our providers (Terms §3.5). Weekly credits that
expire unused are not refunded either (section 1.3).

**3.4 Platform failures.** If you lose credits due to a failure of the
Service—for example, an operation that is charged but not executed because of
an error on our side—**we return them to you as credits**, provided you report
it to us **within 30 days** of when it occurred. This return is in credits, not
money.

**3.5 Billing errors.** If we charge you in error—a duplicate charge, a wrong
amount, a charge after you canceled—**we refund the full amount** of the
error.

**3.6 Credits are not money.** Credits **have no cash value**: they cannot be
exchanged for money and are not refunded except in the cases in this section.

**3.7 What the law requires.** If applicable law grants you a right to a refund
or withdrawal that cannot be limited by contract—for example, as a consumer in
your country of residence (Terms §12.6)—that right prevails over this policy.
