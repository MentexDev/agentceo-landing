# Part I · GDPR (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

## 1. Who Is the Controller and Who Is the Processor

**Klinworks is the controller** of the data of the people who use the Service:
account, billing, support and use of the platform.

**Klinworks is a processor** of the data you enter about your customers,
contacts and suppliers. For that data, **you are the controller**, and we
process it solely on your documented instructions, which are those arising from
your use of the Service and from these legal documents.

## 2. Legal Bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Provision of the Service | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Invoicing and payment collection | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Security and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Improving the Service with aggregated data | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Tax and accounting obligations | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Marketing communications | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |

Where we rely on legitimate interest, we have balanced that interest against
your rights and freedoms. You may request the outcome of that balancing test
from us.

## 3. Your Rights (Arts. 15 to 22)

- **Access** (Art. 15): obtain confirmation and a copy of your data.
- **Rectification** (Art. 16): correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** (Art. 17): the "right to be forgotten," where applicable.
- **Restriction** (Art. 18): restrict processing in certain cases.
- **Portability** (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, commonly used
  format, and transmit it to another controller.
- **Objection** (Art. 21): object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Automated decision-making** (Art. 22): not be subject to decisions based
  solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant
  effects.
- **Withdrawal of consent** (Art. 7(3)): at any time, without affecting the
  lawfulness of prior processing.

**How to exercise them:** write to **privacidad@klinworks.com** stating the
right you are exercising. We will respond within **one month**, extendable by
two further months if the request is complex, in which case we will inform you
within the first month. Exercising your rights is free of charge, except for
manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

**Complaints:** you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in
your country of residence. In Spain, this is the Spanish Data Protection Agency
(Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

## 4. International Transfers (Arts. 44 to 49)

Klinworks processes data in the United States. Transfers from the EEA, the
United Kingdom or Switzerland are covered by the **Standard Contractual
Clauses** approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914,
supplemented by additional technical and organizational measures: encryption in
transit and at rest, role-based access control and minimization of the data
transferred.

You may request information about these safeguards at privacidad@klinworks.com.

## 5. Subprocessors (Arts. 28(2) and 28(4))

The current, complete list, with the status of each entry, is at
[Subprocessors](/en/legal/subprocessors) (the same list shown in the Privacy
Policy):

All of them are subject to data protection obligations equivalent to ours. When
we add a new one that processes personal data, we will update that list and the

## 6. Security Measures (Art. 32)

Encryption in transit and at rest, isolation between organizations enforced in
the database, role-based access control, available two-factor authentication,
audit logging of sensitive operations, and backups. The details are in the
[Security Policy](/en/legal/security).

## 7. Personal Data Breaches (Arts. 33 and 34)

In the event of a breach that poses a risk to individuals' rights, we will
notify the competent supervisory authority **within 72 hours** of becoming
aware of it, and the affected individuals without undue delay where the risk is
high.

Where we act as a processor on your behalf, we will inform you without undue
delay so that you can meet your own notification obligations.

## 8. Return and Deletion upon Termination (Art. 28(3)(g))

Upon termination of the relationship, you have **30 days** to export your
content. After that period, we will delete it, except for what we must retain
under a legal obligation. Backups follow their rotation cycle and are
overwritten within the period stated in the Privacy Policy.

## 9. Audits (Art. 28(3)(h))

We will make available to you the information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with our obligations as a processor. If you need a
signed data processing agreement, write to us at legal@klinworks.com.

# Part II · CCPA / CPRA (California)

## 10. Categories of Personal Information

In the last twelve months, we have collected the following categories, as
classified under California Civil Code §1798.140:

| Category | Do we collect it? | Examples |

| Internet activity | Yes | Use of the Service, access logs, errors |
| Professional information | Yes | Company, role within the organization |
| Precise geolocation data | No | — |
| Protected classifications | No | — |
| Biometric information | No | — |
| Contents of communications | Yes | Conversations with the agents and content you upload |
| Inferences for profiling | No | — |
| Sensitive personal information | Not deliberately | See Privacy Policy, Section 2 |

**Sources:** the data is provided by you, generated by your use of the Service,
or comes from systems you connect.

**Purposes:** those described in Section 3 of the Privacy Policy.

## 11. No Sale or Sharing

**We do not sell personal information**, nor do we "share" it for cross-context
behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We have not
done so in the last twelve months, including with respect to consumers under 16
years of age.

## 12. Your Rights in California

The full notice for California is in the
[Notice to California Residents](/en/legal/california). Summary:

- **Know**: what categories we collect, from where, for what purposes and with
  whom we share them.
- **Access**: obtain a copy of your personal information.
- **Delete**: request its deletion, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
- **Correct**: rectify inaccurate information.
- **Opt out**: of sale or sharing — not applicable, because we do not engage in
  them.
- **Limit the use of sensitive information**: not applicable, because we do not
  use it for purposes that require this limitation.
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not give you a worse service or a different
  price for exercising your rights.

**How to exercise them:** write to **privacidad@klinworks.com**. We will verify
your identity through the account associated with the request. We will respond
within **45 days**, extendable by another 45 days with notice to you.

**Authorized agent:** you may designate someone to act on your behalf, with
written proof of their authorization.
