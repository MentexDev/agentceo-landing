# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs how **Klinworks, LLC**
("Klinworks," "we," "us"), a Delaware (United States) limited liability
company, file no. 10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental
Drive, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713, processes the personal data that you,
as a customer ("you," the "Customer"), enter into or connect to the **Agent
CEO** service (the "Service").

It applies to customers who use the Service to process personal data of third
parties—their customers, contacts, and suppliers—and it meets the requirements
of **Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation** (EU) 2016/679
("GDPR") and equivalent laws. It supplements, and is consistent with, the
[Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms), the
[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy), and the
[GDPR and CCPA Notice](/en/legal/gdpr).

**This document is the published version of the DPA. If you have signed a
version of the DPA with us, the signed version prevails** over this page.

---

## 1. Roles of each party

**1.1 You are the controller.** You decide why and how the personal data of
your customers, contacts, and suppliers is processed, and you are responsible
for having a **legal basis** to process it and for having informed those
people.

**1.2 Klinworks is the processor.** When we process that data to provide the
Service to you, we act **on your behalf** and only on your instructions.

**1.3 What this DPA does not cover.** We process the data of the Service's
users—your account, billing, support, and platform usage—as a **controller**,
in accordance with the Privacy Policy. This DPA does not apply to that data.

## 2. Subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose

**2.1 Subject matter.** The processing of the personal data that the Customer
enters into the Service or that reaches it from the systems the Customer
connects.

**2.2 Duration.** For as long as the Service is provided, plus the return and
deletion period in section 10.

**2.3 Nature.** Hosting, storage, organization, retrieval, modification,
transmission to the systems you connect, and processing by AI agents and
language models, always according to the configuration you set.

**2.4 Purpose.** Solely **to provide the Service to you**: so that your agents
carry out the work of your business that you configure. **We do not use that
data for our own purposes** or to train AI models (Terms §7.3).

## 3. Categories of data and data subjects

**3.1 Data subjects.** The people whose data you enter or connect: your
**customers**, **contacts**, **suppliers**, and, where applicable, other third
parties your business deals with.

**3.2 Categories of data.** Those you decide to process, typically: name,
email address, phone number, company, order and transaction history,
conversations by email, WhatsApp, or other channels, and the content your
agents generate about them.

**3.3 Special categories.** The Service is not designed to process special
categories of data (health, ethnic origin, political opinions, biometrics). If
you enter them, you do so under your own responsibility and must have a legal
basis for doing so (Privacy Policy §2).

## 4. Documented instructions

**4.1 Only your instructions.** We process the data solely on your
**documented instructions**, which are those that derive from the Terms, from
this DPA, and from how you configure and use the Service, including its
integrations and its agents.

**4.2 Unlawful instruction.** If we believe an instruction infringes the GDPR
or another data protection law, we will inform you without delay.

**4.3 Legal obligations.** If the law requires us to process the data in
another way, we will inform you beforehand, unless that same law prohibits it.

## 5. Confidentiality

People authorized to access the data are bound by a **duty of
confidentiality**, whether contractual or statutory. Our staff's access is
limited to what is strictly necessary to operate the Service and respond to
incidents; we do not read your content unless you ask us to, the law requires
it, or it is strictly necessary to investigate serious abuse (Security Policy
§3.4).

## 6. Security (Art. 32)

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk,
including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest.
- Encryption of integration credentials with a key that does not reside in
  the database.
- Isolation between organizations in two layers, database and application,
  verified with automated tests.
- Role-based access control and two-step verification available.
- Audit logging of sensitive operations, and backups.

The details, **including what we do not yet have**, are in the
[Security Policy](/en/legal/security). We may update these measures as long as
they do not reduce the level of protection.

subprocessors that process personal data on your behalf to provide the
Service.

location, and status, is published at
[/en/legal/subprocessors](/en/legal/subprocessors). That page forms part of
this DPA.

**7.3 Same obligations.** We impose on each subprocessor, by contract, data
protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA. We remain liable to you
for their compliance.

subprocessor with [PENDIENTE: days of advance notice, e.g., 30] advance

## 8. Assistance to the controller

**8.1 Data subject rights.** We will help you, insofar as possible and taking
into account the nature of the processing, to respond to your data subjects'
requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and
objection. The Service lets you view, correct, export, and delete data
directly. If a person contacts us about data we process on your behalf, we
will refer them to you without responding on the merits.

**8.2 Other obligations.** We will reasonably assist you with your obligations
regarding security, breach notification, impact assessments, and prior
consultation with the supervisory authority (GDPR Arts. 32 to 36), with the
information available to us.

## 9. Security breaches

If we suffer a security breach affecting the data we process on your behalf,
we will notify you **without undue delay** and within [PENDIENTE: time frame
in hours, e.g., 48 h] of becoming aware of it, and in no event later than the
**72 hours** set out in the Security Policy (§10).

The notification will include, to the extent known to us: what happened, the
categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected, the
likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact for further
information. If we do not have all the information at once, we will provide it
in phases. This allows you to meet your own obligations under GDPR Arts. 33
and 34.

## 10. Return and deletion upon termination

When the Service ends, you have **30 days** to **export** your content (Terms
§9.3). After that period, **we delete** the personal data we process on your
behalf, except what the law requires us to retain. Backups follow their
rotation cycle and are overwritten within the period stated in the Privacy
Policy (§7).

## 11. Audits

We will make available to you the **information reasonably necessary** to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and we will allow for and contribute to
**reasonable audits**, including inspections, conducted by you or by an auditor
you appoint, subject to:

- Reasonable prior notice and agreement on scope, date, and duration.
- A duty of confidentiality on the auditor.
- No access to other customers' data and no compromise of the Service's
  security.

## 13. Liability and order of precedence

**13.1 Liability.** Each party's liability arising from this DPA is governed by
the limits in the Terms (§10), except to the extent the law does not permit
limitation.

**13.2 Precedence.** In matters of data protection, this DPA prevails over the
Terms. A signed version of the DPA prevails over this published version.
