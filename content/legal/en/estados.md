# Privacy Rights in Other U.S. States

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

Several U.S. states have passed their own consumer privacy laws. This document
explains what rights **Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we," "us"), which
operates **Agent CEO** (agentceo.io and app.agentceo.io), grants to residents of
those states, and how to exercise them.

Klinworks is a Delaware (United States) limited liability company, File No.
10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305,
Newark, Delaware 19713.

If you live in California, see the
[Notice to California Residents](/en/legal/california). What data we process
and why is described in the
[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy).

**Contact for exercising your rights: privacidad@klinworks.com**

## 1. Which States This Covers

Among others, **Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and
Montana**, and any other state that has or passes a consumer privacy law.

## 2. Even If the Law Does Not Require It, We Honor These Rights

Many of these laws apply only to businesses that exceed certain **thresholds**
(for example, the number of residents whose data they process, or revenue). It
is possible that some of them **do not apply to us** today.

Even so, **we honor the rights on this page for any U.S. resident who writes to
us**, regardless of the state they live in. We prefer a single high standard
for everyone.

## 3. Your Rights

| Right | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirm and access** | Know whether we process your personal data and receive a copy |
| **Correct** | Rectify inaccurate data |
| **Delete** | Ask us to delete your data, except what the law requires us to retain |
| **Portability** | Receive your data in a commonly used format that you can take elsewhere |
| **Opt out of sale** | Object to the sale of your data |
| **Opt out of targeted advertising** | Object to its use to show you ads based on your activity |
| **Opt out of profiling** | Object to automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you |
| **Non-discrimination** | Not receive worse treatment or a worse price for exercising your rights |

## 4. What We Already Do Not Do, by Default

- **We do not sell** personal data.
- **We do not engage in targeted advertising**: the website does not use
  analytics or advertising cookies.
- **We do not profile you** to make decisions that produce legal or similarly
  significant effects concerning you (Section 9 of the
  [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy)).
- We honor your browser's **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal as a request
  to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.

## 6. How to Exercise Your Rights

**6.1 Request.** Write to **privacidad@klinworks.com** stating which right you
are exercising and which state you reside in. It is free of charge.

**6.2 Verification.** We will verify that it is you before providing,
correcting or deleting data, normally through the email address or account
associated with the request.

**6.3 Authorized agent.** Where permitted by law, another person may act on
your behalf with your written authorization.

**6.4 Timeframe.** We will respond within **45 days**, extendable by another
**45** when necessary, with notice to you before the first period expires.

## 7. If You Disagree: Appeals

If we deny your request, in whole or in part, we will explain why and you may
**appeal**:

1. Write to **privacidad@klinworks.com** with the subject line "Appeal" and the
   reference of your request.
2. A person other than the one who made the original decision will review the
   case.
3. We will respond in writing with the decision and the reasons for it within
   the period set by the law of your state. [PENDIENTE: appeal response period
   committed to by Klinworks.]

If we uphold the denial, you may file a complaint with **your state's Attorney
General's office**. We will tell you how to contact it in our response.
