## 1. What They Are

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser to remember
information between visits. Alongside cookies, we use equivalent technologies
that also store data on your device:

- **`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`**: browser storage we use to keep you
  signed in and to remember interface preferences.
- **IndexedDB**: used by the browser so the application runs more smoothly.

In this policy, "cookies" refers to all of them.

## 2. Exactly What We Use

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary

Without them, the Service does not work. **They do not require consent**
because they are essential to provide a service you have requested.

| What it stores | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session token | Keeps you signed in without asking for your password on every page | Until you sign out or it expires |
| Refresh token | Renews your session without interrupting you | Until you sign out |
| Interface preferences | Remembers the state of the side menu and view settings | Persistent until you delete it |
| Form protection | Prevents cross-site fraudulent submissions | Session |

### 2.2 Functional

They improve your experience without being essential: for example, they
remember the last organization you worked with or the state of panels you have
collapsed.

### 2.3 Analytics

**We currently do not use third-party analytics cookies or advertising tracking
tools.** If we add analytics in the future, we will ask for your prior consent
and update this table before enabling it.

### 2.4 Advertising

**We do not use advertising cookies.** We do not engage in profiling for
advertising purposes, nor do we share identifiers with ad networks.

The providers that support our infrastructure may set their own technical
cookies:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|

| **Supabase** | Management of the authenticated session |

Both are technical in nature and necessary for the Service to operate securely.

## 4. How to Control Them

**4.1 From your browser.** You can block or delete cookies in the settings of
any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that **blocking the
strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in** to the Service.

**4.2 Signing out.** Signing out invalidates the tokens associated with your
session.

**4.3 Active sessions.** Within the Service, in your account settings, you can
see the devices with an open session and sign them out remotely.

**4.4 Privacy signals.** We honor *Global Privacy Control* (GPC) and *Do Not
Track* signals to the extent they apply to non-essential processing. Since we
currently do not use analytics or advertising, there is no non-essential
processing to disable.

## 5. Changes

If we start using cookies in a new category, we will update this policy and,
where required by law, ask for your consent before enabling them. The date in
the header always indicates the latest version.
