# Copyright and DMCA

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

**Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we," "us"), which operates **Agent CEO**
(agentceo.io and app.agentceo.io), respects the intellectual property of
others and asks the same of those who use the Service. This policy explains how
we handle copyright infringement claims under the U.S. **Digital Millennium
Copyright Act** ("DMCA").

Klinworks is a Delaware (United States) limited liability company, file no.
10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305,
Newark, Delaware 19713.

It applies to any rights holder, or anyone acting on their behalf, and to any
customer whose content is the subject of a notice. The
[Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms) prohibit infringing the intellectual
property of others (section 4.2) and explain who owns the content
(section 7).

## 2. How to send us a notice of infringement

If you believe that content hosted or published through Agent CEO infringes
your copyright, send us a written notice that includes, under
**17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3)**:

- Your **signature**, physical or electronic, as the owner or as a person
  authorized to act on their behalf.
- **Identification of the copyrighted work** you claim has been infringed (or,
  if there are several, a representative list).
- **Identification of the material** you claim is infringing and information
  reasonably sufficient to locate it (for example, the exact address of the
  page).
- Your **contact information**: name, address, phone number, and email
  address.
- A statement that you have a **good faith** belief that use of the material is
  not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
- A statement that the information in the notice is **accurate** and, **under
  penalty of perjury**, that you are the owner or are authorized to act on
  their behalf.

An incomplete notice may not be valid. If something is missing, we may ask you
to complete it before we act.

## 3. What we do when we receive it

1. We check that the notice meets the requirements.
2. We expeditiously remove or disable access to the material.
3. We notify the customer responsible for the content and send them a copy of
   the notice, which may include your contact information.

## 4. Counter-notice: if you believe your content was removed by mistake

If your content was removed and you believe it was due to a mistake or
misidentification, you may send us a counter-notice under
**17 U.S.C. §512(g)** that includes:

- Your **signature**, physical or electronic.
- **Identification of the material** removed and the location where it
  appeared before it was removed.
- A statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that you have a good faith belief
  that it was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
- Your name, address, and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the
  **jurisdiction of the federal district court** for your district (or, if you
  reside outside the U.S., any judicial district in which Klinworks may be
  found) and that you will accept service of process from the person who
  submitted the original notice or their agent.

We will forward the counter-notice to the person who submitted the original
notice. If that person does not inform us, within the period set by law, that
they have filed a court action to restrain the infringement, we may restore the
material.

## 5. False notices: be careful

Under **17 U.S.C. §512(f)**, anyone who knowingly materially misrepresents
something in a notice or counter-notice **may be liable for damages** caused,
including costs and attorneys' fees. If you are not sure whether a use
infringes your rights, consult an attorney first.

## 6. Repeat infringers

We will, in appropriate circumstances, terminate the accounts of those who
repeatedly infringe copyrights. We may also limit access or remove content from
any account when we deem it appropriate, in accordance with section 4.3 of the
[Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms).

## 7. AI-generated content

Content that agents generate for you is yours to the extent we hold rights in
it (section 7.4 of the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms)). But **you are the
one who publishes it** and who is responsible for ensuring it does not infringe
the rights of others (section 5.6). If we receive a notice about content you
published, this policy applies just as it does to any other content.

## 8. Other claims

For claims concerning trademarks or rights other than copyright, also write to
us at **legal@klinworks.com** with as much information as possible.
