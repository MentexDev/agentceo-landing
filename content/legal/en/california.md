# Notice to California Residents

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

This notice explains how **Klinworks, LLC** ("Klinworks," "we," "us") processes
the personal information of California residents when they visit the
**agentceo.io** website or use the **Agent CEO** application at app.agentceo.io
(the "Service"), and what rights they have under the **California Consumer
Privacy Act**, as amended by the **California Privacy Rights Act** (together,
the "CCPA").

Klinworks is a Delaware (United States) limited liability company, File No.
10704723, with its registered office at 131 Continental Drive, Suite 305,
Newark, Delaware 19713.

This notice supplements the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy) and Part II of
the [GDPR and CCPA Notice](/en/legal/gdpr). Anything not covered here is
governed by those documents.

**Contact for exercising your rights: privacidad@klinworks.com**

---

## 1. Who This Notice Applies To

It applies to any California resident whose personal information we process
**as a business (controller)**: website visitors, people who join the waitlist
or request a resource, and users of the Service.

It **does not apply** to the data our customers enter into the Service about
their own customers, contacts or suppliers. There, we act as a **service
provider** (processor) to the customer, who decides about that data. If you are
a customer of one of our customers, direct your request to that company; if it
reaches us, we will forward it to them or tell you whom to contact.

## 2. What Information We Collect

### 2.1 On the Website (agentceo.io)

- **Waitlist:** email address, language, source campaign (UTM parameters), the
  page you landed on, the site you came from (referrer), browser (user-agent),
  type of business if you choose to provide it, and the version of the notice
  shown to you when you signed up.
- **Downloadable resources:** email address and the resource you requested.
- **Website assistant:** **we do not store your conversations on our server.**
  They remain only in your browser for 7 days. To prevent abuse, we apply a
  daily cap on questions per IP address, and for that purpose we store a
  **non-reversible digest (hash) of the IP address**, never the IP address in
  plain text.
- **Technical security logs** generated by Cloudflare, our network and
  protection provider.

The website **does not use analytics or advertising cookies**.

### 2.2 In the Application (app.agentceo.io)

Your account data, billing data (payments are processed by Stripe; we do not
store full card numbers), Service usage data and the business content you enter.
The details are in Section 2 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy), and the
list of providers is at [Subprocessors](/en/legal/subprocessors).

## 5. What We Use the Information For

- Managing the waitlist and notifying you of the launch.
- Sending you the resource you requested.
- Understanding which campaigns and pages generate interest, without

- Providing, billing for and supporting the Service.

- Complying with legal and tax obligations.

The purposes of the Service are detailed in Section 3 of the
[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy).

## 6. We Neither Sell Nor Share

**We do not sell personal information**, nor do we **"share"** it for
cross-context behavioral advertising, as the CCPA defines those terms. This
includes consumers under 16 years of age.

**Global Privacy Control (GPC).** If your browser sends the GPC signal, we treat
it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal
information, without you having to do anything else.

You can also use the
[Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information](/en/do-not-sell-or-share/) page.

## 7. Who We Disclose It To

We disclose personal information only to **service providers** that help us
operate the website and the Service (infrastructure, database, payments, email,
AI models), under contracts that prohibit them from using it for their own
purposes. The current list is at [Subprocessors](/en/legal/subprocessors).

In addition, we may disclose it to authorities when required by law, or to
advisors or acquirers in a corporate transaction, as set out in Section 5 of
the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy).

## 9. Your Rights

As a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know and access:** what personal information we hold about you, where it
  comes from, what we use it for and with whom we share it, and receive a copy.
- **Delete:** ask us to delete it, subject to the exceptions permitted by law
  (for example, what we must retain under tax obligations).
- **Correct:** rectify inaccurate information.
- **Opt out of sale or sharing:** object to the sale or sharing of your
  information. We currently do neither, but the right is still yours.
- **Limit the use of sensitive information:** ask that it be used only for what
  is strictly necessary to provide the Service to you.
- **Non-discrimination:** we will not give you a worse service or a different
  price for exercising any of these rights.

## 10. How to Exercise Them

**10.1 Request.** Write to **privacidad@klinworks.com** stating which right you
are exercising. It is free of charge.

**10.2 Verification.** Before providing or deleting information, we will verify
that it is you. We will normally ask you to reply from the email address you
signed up with or to sign in to your account. We will use the verification data
only for that purpose.

**10.3 Authorized agent.** You may ask another person to act on your behalf. We
will ask them for a permission signed by you and may ask you to confirm your
identity directly with us.

**10.4 Timeframe.** We will confirm receipt and respond within **45 days**,
extendable by another **45** when necessary, with notice to you before the
first period expires.

## 11. Children

The Service is not directed to minors and, in particular, **we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13** (federal COPPA law). If we
discover that we have it, we delete it. The
[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy) also sets the minimum age to use the
Service.

## 12. Changes to This Notice

We will review it at least once a year and whenever what we collect or how we
use it changes. The date in the header indicates the latest version.
